About

Where engineering meets code

I'm a junior at Vernon Hills High School, carrying a 4.00 unweighted GPA through four AP courses and a dual-credit class. My work sits at the intersection of mechanical design and software — I model and prototype in CAD, then bring the same problem-solving into Java and Python.

Competitively, I build for Science Olympiad — flight, helicopter, electric vehicle, and wheeled vehicle events — and I placed 2nd in a school hackathon building VERA, an AI accessibility tool. Outside the build table, I'm Vice President of hackVH, a member of Math Team, and a brown belt in karate with four competition medals.

I'm looking toward mechanical engineering and computer science internships where I can keep working on problems that need both a CAD model and a codebase to solve.
